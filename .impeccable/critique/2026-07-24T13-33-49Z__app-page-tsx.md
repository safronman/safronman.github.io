## Design Specificity Verdict

The portfolio feels authored for a technical educator, not interchangeable: the night-studio hero, single portrait, ruled proof inventory and restrained signal blue fit a credible, engineering-minded teaching practice. The strongest work is the move from claim to real artefacts. Its weak point is editorial focus: after the selected work, the page repeats capability and process explanations long enough that the proof-first story loses momentum.

The deterministic scan found 13 advisory `design-system-font-size` findings across contact, hero, learning approach, selected work, teaching practices and technical foundation CSS. Most look like intentional fluid type refinements, but the type scale in CSS and DESIGN.md should be reconciled. No colour, semantic, image or component findings were reported.

## Overall Impression

This is ready to show: it looks calm, specific and credible. The highest-value next step is not visual reinvention; it is making the first minute of recruiter evaluation shorter and more decisive.

## What's Working

- Proof is real and inspectable: videos, presentations and materials are linked from `app/selected-work/selected-work.tsx` instead of relying on unsupported claims.
- The visual language is consistent: flat surfaces, precise dividers and a single blue action colour sustain the technical-editorial character.
- Implementation fundamentals are solid: responsive `next/image` usage, deferred YouTube embed, descriptive image text, visible focus styles and reduced-motion support in several interactive sections.

## Priority Issues

### [P1] The proof catalogue is broader than a hiring manager needs

**Why it matters:** Four featured works, six additional artefacts and multiple links create comparison work before the visitor can decide whether to contact you.

**Fix:** Keep three flagship artefacts first. For each, state role, audience, delivered artefact and one clearly labelled inspection action. Move the rest into a compact optional archive.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable distill`

### [P1] The strongest proof does not consistently state an outcome or ownership

**Why it matters:** Current text explains what you do, but a recruiter still has to infer your exact role, scope and the value of each artefact.

**Fix:** Add concise factual labels to featured work: «Роль», «Для кого», «Что создано», «Смотреть / читать». Use destination-specific labels rather than generic «Открыть».

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable clarify`

### [P2] The middle of the page repeats the same claim in three sections

**Why it matters:** `learning-approach`, `teaching-practices` and `technical-foundation` all describe capability after visitors have already seen evidence. This creates a reading valley before contact.

**Fix:** Keep one compact «Как работаю» section and one short «Что могу преподавать». Let the selected work do the rest of the persuasion.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable distill`

### [P2] Accessibility and mobile navigation need a final pass

**Why it matters:** There is no skip link, motion reduction is incomplete in hero/video controls, and the narrow header hides its horizontal scrollbar despite overflow. This affects keyboard, motion-sensitive and mobile visitors.

**Fix:** Add a skip link to the main content, complete `prefers-reduced-motion` rules and replace the scrolling mobile navigation with an accessible compact menu or section picker.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable harden`, then `$impeccable adapt`

### [P3] Design documentation and repository checks are out of sync

**Why it matters:** The type scale differs from DESIGN.md in 13 places. `pnpm.cmd lint` also reports formatting in `learning-approach.tsx` and `technical-foundation.tsx`; its cache-file report is tooling noise, not application quality.

**Fix:** Format the two source files, then either document accepted type steps or align them with DESIGN.md.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable document`

## Persona Red Flags

### Jordan — hiring manager / first-time visitor

Jordan can understand the promise quickly, but has to evaluate too many artefacts without a «start here» recommendation or clear role/outcome labels.

### Casey — time-poor education leader on mobile

Casey reaches real proof early, then scrolls through several explanatory sections before another contact invitation. The hidden-overflow navigation gives little hint that more section links exist.

### Sam — keyboard and screen-reader user

Sam benefits from meaningful controls and visible focus styles, but must traverse the fixed header before the main content and may still encounter animation in hero/video controls when requesting reduced motion.

## Minor Observations

- Keep external destination types explicit: video, presentation, material or website.
- Consider one restrained CTA immediately after selected work: «Обсудить программу или роль».
- Replace scroll-time layout reads in the header scroll spy with `IntersectionObserver` when performance work is next scheduled.
