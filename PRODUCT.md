# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary audience: hiring managers and leaders of education products who are assessing a candidate for a Technical Educator role. They need a concise, credible view of how the candidate researches technical subjects, structures learning, and produces usable educational materials. Secondary audiences include teams seeking an author of technical programmes, teaching leads, and engineers evaluating the depth of technical expertise.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio helps education companies evaluate a Technical Educator whose strongest demonstrated background is in frontend development and who can extend that capability to new technical areas. Success is an informed hiring decision and a direct offer or conversation initiated through Telegram.

## Positioning

Teach, record videos, create programmes, and lead technical education—from researching a subject to setting a consistent standard for materials and teaching teams.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: send a role proposal via Telegram.
- Secondary CTA: review selected projects and their linked videos or presentations before making contact.
- Alternative contact channel: email.
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: Quickly master complex technical subjects and turn them into clear, practical learning experiences.
- Belief ladder: this is a technically credible educator; their teaching work is structured and practical; their existing materials demonstrate the quality; they can learn and teach relevant new tools; contacting them is worthwhile.
- Proof on hand: publicly available course videos, project links, and presentations referenced from the selected case studies. Do not add unverified individual-performance metrics, testimonials, or logos.

## Brand Personality

Strict, expert, engineering-minded. The site should feel assured and precise rather than playful or school-like.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic teacher-portfolio templates: childlike palettes, playful classroom typography, scrapbook/CV collages, oversized decorative illustration, and visual noise. Do not reduce the educator to a single frontend specialty even though frontend is the strongest current proof of expertise.

## Design Principles

1. Let tangible teaching artefacts make the case before the contact prompt.
2. Make technical range credible without overstating personal impact.
3. Prefer structured clarity over generic personal-brand decoration.
4. Give hiring managers a fast path from claim to evidence to contact.
5. Treat frontend expertise as a foundation for adaptable technical education, not a constraint.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Provide a responsive, readable experience with strong text contrast and support for reduced motion. No additional accessibility requirements have been specified.
